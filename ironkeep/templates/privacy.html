Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Ironkeep is a Discord bot and website that helps guilds follow new members through a trial period. This page explains what Ironkeep stores about you, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who is responsible

Ironkeep is run by [name of the person or company], [country]. For any question or request about your data, email [privacy@ironkeep.gg]. We answer within 30 days.

What we store

WhoWhatWhere it comes from
Members of a server that uses IronkeepDiscord user ID, username, display name, avatar, the date you joined, and which of the guild's chosen roles you haveDiscord, through the bot
People on a trialTrial start and end date, whether a reminder was sent, the verdict, and a timeline of what happened during the trialThe bot, and recruiters of that guild
People on a trialObservations written by the guild's recruiters, for example about attendance or how you fit in the groupRecruiters of that guild
Recruiters and server admins who log inDiscord user ID, username, avatar, and which servers you're inDiscord login

We don't read messages, voice activity or direct messages. We don't store email addresses or passwords.

Why, and on what basis

Ironkeep uses this data only to run a guild's recruitment process: posting the messages the guild has set up, tracking trial dates, and helping recruiters decide whether to accept someone. The legal basis is legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): the guild's interest in running its trials in an organised way, weighed against your interests. Observations are only visible to that guild's recruiters and to the Ironkeep administrator. They are never shown to other guilds.

How long we keep it

  • Closed trials, including observations and the timeline, are deleted 12 months after the verdict.
  • If a guild removes the bot, or its request is rejected, all data for that guild is deleted after 30 days.
  • Error logs are kept for 90 days.
  • Login sessions end after 30 days, or when you log out.

Who processes it for us

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) hosts the website, the bot and the database. Data stays in the EU.
  • Discord provides login and the data the bot receives. Discord's own privacy policy applies to your Discord account.

We don't sell data and don't share it with anyone else.

Cookies

The website uses one cookie to keep you logged in. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of everything Ironkeep stores about you, including observations, and ask us to correct or delete it. You can also object to the processing. Email [privacy@ironkeep.gg] with your Discord user ID. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority; in the Netherlands that's the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and tell guild admins in Discord.
